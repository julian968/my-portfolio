Rotate roles halfway through so everyone practices asking questions. The Scribe owns the final output document.

02 30-Minute Structure
0:00
Define the Outcome
Before asking anything — what does success look like? What's the artifact? What's the business impact? Write it down first.
5:00
Discovery Round 1
SE asks, Prospect answers, Scribe captures. Focus on systems, data sources, and current state.
15:00
Rotate Roles + Round 2
Swap SE ↔ Prospect. New SE picks up where the last left off — go deeper on gaps and edge cases.
25:00
Synthesis + Prep Readout
Scribe leads. Fill in the output template. Identify your top 3 open questions. Get ready to present back.
03 Discovery Areas & Starter Questions

Use these as starting points — go deeper when something sounds interesting or unclear. Check off questions as you cover them. Rotate through categories.

01Who currently owns this workflow — is it one person or shared across a team?
02What happens if this workflow breaks or is late — what's the downstream business impact?
03How long does this take today, and how much of it is manual?
04Who are the stakeholders and what do they actually do with the output?
05What does success look like — what's different when this workflow is working perfectly?
06Is there a version of this that already partially exists, or is it fully manual today?
01What tools or platforms are involved in this workflow today?
02Does data move between systems automatically or is someone copying it manually?
03Are there any systems that are restricted, read-only, or require special access?
04Is Notion already involved — and if so, where in the chain does it sit?
05Are there any approval steps or human-in-the-loop checkpoints built in?
06What authentication or permissions model does each system use?
01Where does the source data live — one system or multiple?
02What specific fields or data points are required in the output?
03Is the data clean and structured, or does it need transformation before it's usable?
04Are there filters — date ranges, deal stages, territories — that scope what gets included?
05How frequently does the data update — real-time, daily, on a trigger?
06What happens if a required data field is missing or null?
01What does the final artifact actually look like — a Notion page, a spreadsheet, a Slack message?
02Is the output consumed by a human, another system, or both?
03Does the output need to follow a specific template or format?
04How is the output delivered — pushed to the recipient, or pulled by them?
05Does anyone need to review or approve the output before it's distributed?
06Is there a record kept of previous outputs — versioning, history, audit trail?
01What happens if one of the source systems is down — does the workflow fail silently or loudly?
02Are there exceptions or one-off cases that the normal process doesn't handle well?
03Does the workflow behave differently at end-of-quarter, or during high-volume periods?
04Are there any compliance, security, or data residency constraints we need to design around?
05If a step fails partway through, does it retry — or does someone have to restart manually?
